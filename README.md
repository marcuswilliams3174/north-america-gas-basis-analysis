# North American Natural Gas Basis Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes the structural relationship between North American natural gas pricing benchmarks to understand basis behavior, volatility regimes, and market-driven pricing dislocations.

## Objective
To evaluate how regional supply-demand dynamics and infrastructure constraints impact natural gas pricing behavior across AECO and Henry Hub benchmarks.

## Key Areas of Focus
- Basis spread behavior (AECO vs Henry Hub)
- Volatility clustering in gas markets
- Seasonal pricing dynamics
- Market structure and constraint-driven dislocations

## Tools Used
Python, Pandas, Matplotlib

## Status
In development — building data ingestion and analysis framework
